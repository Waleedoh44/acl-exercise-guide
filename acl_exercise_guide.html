🏋️ ACL-Safe Hypertrophy Program

5 Days/Week • Bodyweight + Bands + Dumbbells + Ankle Weights
⚠️ Daily Rule: Pain ≤ 2/10 during & after • No next-day swelling • Tempo matters more than load
🔹 ACTIVATION (Do Daily)
1. Terminal Knee Extension
2 sets × 20 reps
• Slow squeeze at lockout
2. Spanish Squat Hold
2 sets × 30-45 sec
• Band behind knees
🔹 PRIMARY QUAD WORK (Do Daily)
3. Goblet Squat (Dumbbells or sand weight)
4 sets × 6-10 reps
• 3-4 sec slow lowering
• Heels slightly elevated if needed
OR(3): Dumbbells Sit-to-Stand
Same sets & reps
• Controlled stand from chair
🔹 QUAD ISOLATION (Key for Size)
4. Seated Leg Extension
3 sets × 12-15 reps
• 2-3 sec pause at full stretch
• Final reps should burn, not hurt
👉 Main quad-builder at home
🔹 HAMSTRING STRENGTH
5. Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift
3-4 sets × 8-12 reps
• 3 sec eccentric
• Stop just before knee strain
• Back is Straight not Arc
🔹 HAMSTRING ISOLATION
6. Hamstring Curl (with Bands)
3 sets × 10-15 reps
• Slow return
• Keep hips extended
🔹 UNILATERAL / CONTROL
7. Split Squat
3 sets × 8-12 reps/leg
• Upright torso
• Short stride
• Add dumbbells when ready
🔹 ISOMETRIC FINISHERS (Joint-Friendly)
8. Wall Sit
2 sets × 40-60 sec
• 90° knee angle
• Back flat against wall
9. Hamstring Bridge Hold
2 sets × 30-45 sec
• heels elevated if possible
